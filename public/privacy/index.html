Harbe Cut · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Harbe Cut is a personal desktop video-editing application made and used by Harrison B. Goldberg ("I", "me") to edit, publish and review videos for his own Instagram account. It is not offered to the public. This page explains what data the app accesses through Meta's Instagram API and what happens to it.

What the app accesses

Only for the Instagram professional account that I connect and own:

  • Basic account information: username, account ID, follower and media counts.
  • My own posts and reels: captions, timestamps, links, media files and thumbnails.
  • Insights for my own posts: views, reach, likes, comments, shares, saves, watch time and skip rate.
  • Comments on my own posts, so I can read and reply to them from the app.
  • Publishing: posting videos I made to my own account, only when I click Publish.

The app does not access other people's accounts, messages, contacts or any data unrelated to my own posts.

How the data is used and stored

  • The data is used only to edit my videos, see how they perform and improve future videos.
  • It is stored locally on my own computers. The Instagram access token is stored only on the device where it was entered.
  • When I publish a video, the file is uploaded to a temporary private storage link so Instagram's servers can fetch it. The file is deleted from that storage right after publishing.
  • The data is not sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.
  • Commenters' public usernames and comment text may be shown inside the app while I reply to them. Nothing else about them is collected.

Data deletion instructions

To remove Harbe Cut's access and delete your data:

  • In Instagram, go to Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites (or Accounts Center → Your information and permissions) and remove Harbe Cut. This immediately revokes the app's access.
  • To have any data the app stored about your account or comments deleted, email harry@harbegold.com with the subject "Harbe Cut data deletion". It will be deleted within 30 days and you will get a confirmation.
