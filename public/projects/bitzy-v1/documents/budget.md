# Bitzy V1 budget and build status

**The prototype is built. A reconciled total build cost is not established.**

The power/wiring master list records prices from August 31 to September 1, 2026. It mixes previously owned hardware, ordered packs, optional stock and deferred equipment. It predates the reported MS24 replacements and does not capture every mechanical part, material, toolhead, replacement or delivery charge. Its historical procurement quantities must not be treated as the cost of the installed robot.

These are selected quoted item prices from the private power/wiring ledger, not a complete bill of materials, current quotation, actual-spend reconciliation or sum for the built arm. Tax and shipping are not included in these references. The $199.99 dedicated supply was marked deferred because an existing SPS-3010 was used for commissioning. Owned hardware is not assigned zero replacement value.

## Current work

V1 remains an active built prototype. Spending and engineering priorities are documenting the as-built configuration, qualifying the existing tooling, investigating intermittent control/electrical behavior and measuring positioning and load performance. The separate motor-variant budget is excluded from this V1 release.
