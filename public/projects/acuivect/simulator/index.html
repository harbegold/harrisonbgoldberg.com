YOUR VIRTUAL LAB

Operate. Inspect. Understand.

Start with the CAD reference mode. It follows the designed motion precisely. Dynamics adds a reduced MuJoCo axis model with adjustable, unmeasured mechanical assumptions.

Operate

Use +/− to jog, or type an axis position and press Enter. Home retracts Z before seeking X/Y switches. Store and pick up transfer the cartridge only at its designed dock.

Explore

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom and right-drag to pan. Click a part for its source and material. Pick a phantom target, set the depth and run an insertion cycle.

Space Emergency stop · P Pause/resume · R Reset camera · Esc Clear selection
